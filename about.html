Our story

About Switchback.

Inspired by North Carolina trails and weekends spent somewhere outside.

How it started

Switchback Supply Co. started with a familiar routine: picking a trail, checking the weather, and figuring out what to pack. Sometimes that meant a short hike close to Charlotte. Other times, it meant heading toward the mountains with a tent and a few days to spare.

That routine shaped our collection. We focus on the things that earn a place in a pack: comfortable layers, shelter for the night, and the smaller essentials that are easy to overlook.

Our mission

Our mission is to make preparing for a hike or camping trip more straightforward. We bring hiking gear, camping equipment, and trail essentials together in a focused collection with clear descriptions.

Whether you are planning your first overnight trip or replacing a well-used piece of gear, we want to help you find what fits your plans.
